Contact

The lab is based in the Frankel Cardiovascular Center in Ann Arbor. Most inquiries are best directed to one of the routes below.

Inquiries

Proposals for collaboration, questions about our work, and requests for data or code are welcome at vlmurthy@med.umich.edu. Press and speaking invitations may be sent to the same address.

Positions

Open postdoctoral, computational, and clinical research positions are listed on the Join Us page. Applications for those roles must be submitted through the University of Michigan careers site linked from each posting; they cannot be accepted by email. If no current posting matches your interests, you are welcome to write to vlmurthy@med.umich.edu with a CV and a short note about them.

Patient care

We cannot give medical advice, review records, or schedule visits by email, and this address is not a secure or monitored channel for health information. To reach a University of Michigan Health cardiologist, please call the Frankel Cardiovascular Center at 888-287-1082 or contact your own physician. In an emergency, call 911.
